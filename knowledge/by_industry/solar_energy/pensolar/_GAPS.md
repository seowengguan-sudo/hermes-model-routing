## High priority (feed next run)
1. **CAS turnaround time** (not fee — fee is known RM1k-20k). How many weeks does TNB take to
   return a Connection Assessment Study for 72kW-1MW, and what is the rejection/derate rate?
   This is the largest unquantified block inside the C&I 3-5mo window. KPI: timeline adherence.
2. **Certified crew / Competent Person (CP) supply in Peninsular MY.** How many registered CPs and
   SEDA-registered service providers exist, and what is the hourly/day rate trend? Run 2 concluded
   labour — not modules — is the 2027-29 bottleneck; that thesis is unverified.
   KPI: procurement lead time + install cost per kW.
3. **BESS mandate status for >72kWp SELCO after the 31 Dec 2025 deferral.** Did it take effect,
   get deferred again, or get replaced by the standby charge? Directly changes C&I system cost
   and whether standby charge is avoidable. KPI: install cost per kW.
4. **Installed cost per kW breakdown for MY C&I rooftop** (RM/Wp split: module / inverter / racking /
   labour / fees). Only the RM100k-500k total envelope is known — no per-kW structure to benchmark
   quotes or find margin. KPI: install cost per kW.
